For nearly two years I was the only Android engineer on Nobifi — the DeFi suite inside one of Iran's largest exchanges, with millions of installs. Loans, credit, the debit card, staking, yield farming. One person, one surface, end to end.

## Ownership changes how you build

When there's no one to hand the hard part to, you stop writing code you don't understand. You over-invest in the boring foundations — architecture, tooling, generators — because every shortcut becomes your problem at 2am. Ownership is the best teacher of taste I've found.

> Owning a surface end-to-end teaches you that 'done' and 'shipped' are different words for a reason.

- You feel every architectural decision, good and bad, within a quarter.
- Tooling stops being a nice-to-have and becomes survival.
- You learn to say no — because every yes is yours to maintain.

I wouldn't trade those two years. They're the reason I now reach for determinism and generators by default — not because it's fashionable, but because I've felt the cost of the alternative.
