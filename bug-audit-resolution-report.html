The three judgment calls (not-a-bug)

These are where re-verification overrode the audit. Each was decided against the ground-truth artifact, not the report's wording.

BUG-006 — date format is faithful, not wrong

The audit claimed the design wants "Jun 8". The canonical design bundle disagrees:

$ grep -oE '(Jan|…|Dec) 0[1-9], 20..' "Ehsan Koolivand - Blog.html"
Apr 02, 2026 · Jun 02, 2026 · May 06, 2026 → design zero-pads. {d.day:02d} is correct.

BUG-012 — table-row absorption is GFM, by spec

Traced against cmark-gfm: a table continues on every non-blank line until a blank line or block structure. A pipe-bearing prose line under a table is a row in GFM too, and there's no syntactic discriminator from a borderless body row — which is exactly why GFM mandates the blank-line boundary the committed content already uses.

BUG-014 — the design has one heading style on purpose

The bundle's only <h3> is a grid-card title at font-size:20px; article bodies use a single clamp(22px,3vw,30px) style. Semantics and citation anchors (<h3>/<h4> tags, unique ids) are already right. Inventing level sizes is outside the v1.4.0 sanctioned-styling exception (code / callouts / footnotes / line-emphasis / quote-table — not headings).
